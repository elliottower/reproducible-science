# Descriptive: what fraction of published numbers can be traced

**Retired.** The design required two coders annotating forty to eighty values in each
of sixty articles before any artifact was opened. That annotation is the whole cost of
the study and it was never affordable for one person, so it was not run and the
registration and codebook have been deleted rather than left standing as a plan nobody
intends to execute.

What survives is the part that needed no annotation. `frame.json` holds 215 ReScience
articles with 60 drawn by a recorded seed, `published.bib` is the vendored
bibliography, and `snapshots.json` records that all 60 resolve to a pinned artifact.
The scanner runs against them without a coder, and `DEV_LOG.md` and `STATUS.md` carry
what it found.
