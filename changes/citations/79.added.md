**[citations] `citations fetch` downloads the sources a claims directory pins.** A public repository carries each source's sha256 and not the source, so a reader who cloned one had every pin and no file, and `verify` answered `unchecked` for every quotation. `fetch` tries the URL the claims file records, the arXiv PDF, and the open-access locations Europe PMC and OpenAlex list for the DOI, and installs a download only when its sha256 is the pinned one; bytes that differ are reported as `differs` and never written. On a fresh clone of one public study repository, 27 of 94 sources came back byte-identical and 58 of 175 quotations became checkable where none had been.
