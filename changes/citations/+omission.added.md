**A `not found` quotation whose every word is in the source says so.** A quotation made of two or more stretches of the source, in the source's order, with source text left out between them and nothing marking the gap, is still `not found` and still fails. Its result now carries `reason = "omission"` and one gap for each place text was left out, with the position in the quotation and the number of source characters skipped; the report shows both sides of each gap and the `not found` row counts these separately. Every piece must match exactly under the existing folding, begin and end on a word boundary and be at least 20 characters, so a changed word or digit, pieces in another order and a short fragment are a plain `not found` as before.
