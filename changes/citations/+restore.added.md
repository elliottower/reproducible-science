**`citations restore` writes the source's passage for a quotation that leaves text out, as a separate record.** It applies only to an omission that fits the source in exactly one way and leaves out at most `--max-omitted-tokens` tokens, 1 by default. The claims file it is given is never edited; the derived claim goes in `<name>.restored.yaml` beside it, with the original quotation, the bounded passage in the source's own characters, the source and text digests, the offsets of the passage and of each omitted stretch, the rule and its limits, and the software version. `verify` counts restored quotations on a line of their own.
