**A passage no extractor reads as the page prints it can be pinned with a recorded correction.** `citations pin --correct PASSAGE --page N --reads TEXT [--cause NOTE]` writes a `correction` beside the quotation, naming the extractor and its version, the sha256 of the source's bytes and of the extractor's text, and what the extractor produced there; the extracted text is not modified. `citations verify` reports a quotation found in the correction as `corrected`, a new outcome counted apart from `found` in the text report and under `counts` in `--json`, and listed in the text report with what the correction changes (`"eciency" → "efficiency"`), the quotation added under `--verbose`, and in the `corrected` list in `--json` with `reads`, `passage`, `diff` and `flags`; `pin --correct` prints the same difference and flags when it writes and under `--check`. A correction whose changed words differ in a number (its digits, a minus sign before a digit, or a number word from `zero` to `billion`, `half`, `twice`, `double`), in a comparison (`<`, `>`, `=`, `≤`, `≥`, with `<=` and `>=` read as `≤` and `≥`), or in how many negations (`not`, `no`, `never`, `without`, `cannot`, `n't`) or hedges (`may`, `might`) they hold carries a warning saying so, which fails nothing; the flags count words, so `increases` corrected to `decreases` raises none and is seen in the difference. A correction applies where the source's bytes are the recorded ones, `reads` occurs exactly once on the recorded page of this run's reading, and `passage` holds the quotation and, overlapping it, a stretch within a difflib ratio of 0.8 of `reads`, found by one alignment anchored on their longest shared run; the quotation must lie within that stretch, to 3 characters at either end. `reads` is 12 to 200 characters and `passage` at most 400, white space removed, checked before anything is searched. Where `reads` was counted in the whole text, under a declared `extract_cmd` or a source without pages, the result carries `page unchecked`. A quotation carrying `correction` twice, or a correction carrying one of its keys twice, is refused when its file is read, and a key spelled `corrections` is listed as not applied. The extractor, its version and `derived_sha256` decide nothing except for a built-in extractor; where they differ from this run the report says under which reading the correction was applied and under which it was recorded. A correction that does not apply is listed with why (`unapplied_corrections` in `--json`), and one whose source text now holds the quotation is reported as possibly unneeded (`unneeded_corrections`). `pin --correct` refuses a quotation the extractor's text already holds and a correction `verify` would not apply. `repro verify` grades `corrected` under the new `Policy.quotation_corrected`: a warning under `publication` and `strict`, ignored under `exploratory`; where a project makes it an error, the citations line says `FAILED under policy`. Under every policy it names each corrected quotation with its flags and the `citations verify` command that shows the difference.
