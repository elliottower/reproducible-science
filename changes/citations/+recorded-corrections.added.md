**A passage no extractor reads as the page prints it can be pinned with a recorded correction.** `citations pin --correct PASSAGE --page N --reads TEXT [--cause NOTE]` writes a `correction` beside the quotation, naming the extractor and its version, the sha256 of the source's bytes and of the extractor's text, and what the extractor produced there; the extracted text is not modified. `citations verify` reports a quotation found in the correction as `corrected`, a new outcome counted apart from `found` in the text report and under `counts` in `--json`, and named under `--verbose` and in the `corrected` list in `--json`. A correction applies where the source's bytes are the recorded ones, `reads` occurs exactly once on the recorded page of this run's reading, and `passage` holds the quotation and, overlapping it, a stretch within a difflib ratio of 0.8 of a `reads` of at least 12 characters; the quotation must lie within that stretch, to 3 characters at either end. The extractor, its version and `derived_sha256` decide nothing except for a built-in extractor; where they differ from this run the report says under which reading the correction was applied and under which it was recorded. A correction that does not apply is listed with why (`unapplied_corrections` in `--json`), and one whose source text now holds the quotation is reported as possibly unneeded (`unneeded_corrections`). `pin --correct` refuses a quotation the extractor's text already holds and a correction `verify` would not apply. `repro verify` grades `corrected` under the new `Policy.quotation_corrected`: a warning under `publication` and `strict`, ignored under `exploratory`; where a project makes it an error, the citations line says `FAILED under policy`.
