**A passage no extractor reads as the page prints it can be pinned with a recorded correction.** `citations pin --correct PASSAGE --page N --reads TEXT [--cause NOTE]` writes a `correction` beside the quotation, naming the extractor and its version, the sha256 of the source's bytes and of the extractor's text, and what the extractor produced there; the extracted text is not modified. `citations verify` reports a quotation found in the correction as `corrected`, a new outcome counted apart from `found` in the text report and under `counts` in `--json`. A correction recorded against other bytes, another extractor or version, or other extracted text is reported as stale and not applied, and one whose source text now holds the quotation is reported as possibly unneeded (`stale_corrections` and `unneeded_corrections` in `--json`). `pin --correct` refuses a quotation the extractor's text already holds. `repro verify` grades `corrected` under the new `Policy.quotation_corrected`: a warning under `publication` and `strict`, ignored under `exploratory`.
