**`citations audit` compares the venue.** An entry's `journal` or `booktitle`, or its `series` where it names neither, is compared with every name the record gives the venue: Crossref's container titles, short titles and event name and acronym, PubMed's journal title and ISO abbreviation, and the venue and alternate names Semantic Scholar and OpenAlex return. A wrong venue is a disagreement like a wrong year, reported as `venue: ours 'Nature' vs registry 'Nature Communications'`, and fails `--strict`. Names are matched after case, punctuation, stop words, years and ordinals are folded away, as the same words, word-for-word abbreviations (`Nat. Commun.`), acronyms (`ICML`, `KDD '16`), or a small alias table for the ML, NLP, vision, speech and security venues no rule connects (`NeurIPS`, `NAACL-HLT`, `ICASSP`, `CCS`). A derivative journal (`BMJ Open`, `Cell Reports`, `PNAS Nexus`) is not its parent, and a Findings volume is compared by its conference. A venue that cannot be settled is counted under `venue n/c` and never as a disagreement: no venue on one side, a preprint on either side, a workshop or findings volume against the main track, an abbreviation the table does not expand, names that partly agree, a garbled registry name, or a mismatch the other names might undo when OpenAlex or Semantic Scholar did not answer. Before a venue is reported as different, or when the record names only arXiv, OpenAlex and Semantic Scholar are asked for the venue's other names. The Semantic Scholar title search now asks for `publicationVenue` and `journal`, so a cached search from an earlier version is asked again once.
