**A passage that wraps inside a column of a two-column PDF is found by reading the column.** Where the gutter is narrow, `pdftotext -layout`, poppler's reading order, pypdf and pdfplumber all join each line of the left column to the line beside it, and a sentence that wraps inside a column resolved under none of them, so it was reported `not found` or had to be quoted one line at a time. Before a passage is called `not found`, `citations verify` now also reads the PDF with `pdftotext -tsv`, splits each line at the page's gutter and reads each column in turn, and records a passage found that way as a fallback naming `pdftotext -tsv, by column`. A page with no gutter at least six points wide is read top to bottom. The reading is not part of `--triangulate`, does not apply to a source that declares `extract_cmd`, and needs poppler 22.08 or later; with an older poppler it is skipped.
