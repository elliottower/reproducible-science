**A quotation that begins or ends at a hyphen its source puts at a line break carries the warning `line-end hyphen`.** The second reading of a source keeps such a hyphen, so a quotation ending on `publi` in a source printing `publi-` over `cations` is not called `truncated`, and the text cannot say whether the hyphen splits one word, which the quotation then cuts, or ends a line beside an unrelated one. Where one reading cuts a word and the other does not, `citations verify` now says so with this warning, lists the quotation under it with `--verbose` and in `--json`, and `repro verify` reports it as `line_end_hyphen`. It changes no outcome and no exit code.
