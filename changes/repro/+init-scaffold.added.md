**`repro init` in a project with result files and a manuscript and no `repro.yaml` also drafts its claims, and verifies none of them.** It writes `repro.draft.yaml` and `open-items.json` at the top of the project, in the format `claims from-codecheck`, `claims from-corebench` and `claims from-clawbio` write. The draft pins the result files and the manuscript `repro verify` finds with no manifest, under the ids `repro manifest init` gives them, and declares one claim per number the manuscript prints, with its line as the text and no assertion. Each claim has one item asking for the address of its value, listing as candidates every place the result files hold the number, as `repro verify` compares it with no manifest. An item is `unique` where exactly one candidate passes the CORE-Bench reader's guards; a candidate in an index-like column or key never passes. `init` prints `scaffolded, nothing verified: N numbers left open, M with one candidate`, and as the next step to fill the items with an agent (the `open-items` skill, or the tools of `repro mcp`), review the draft and rename it to `repro.yaml`. The draft fails `strict` until filled. No starter `repro.yaml` is written beside a draft, so there is one manifest to rename; with nothing to scaffold, or with `--no-scaffold`, the starter is written as before. Where `repro.yaml` exists `init` scaffolds nothing and says so; it replaces neither file without `--force`; `--no-scaffold` skips the scan. `repro manifest init` is unchanged. `claims.open_items.write_draft` takes `overwrite`, and `claims.starter.scan` returns the survey with the numbers it compared.
