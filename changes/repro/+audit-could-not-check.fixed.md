**`repro audit` counts `citations audit`'s `could not check` row under what the step could not establish**, as it counted the `unresolved` row that row replaces.

**The MCP server's `citations_audit` says why an entry could not be checked.** `why_not` names the services that did not answer (throttled, refused or offline: ask again later) and those that rejected the query as malformed (asking again changes nothing), read from the entry's `refused` and `rejected`, where it had been the bare status `could not check`.

**The MCP server's `citations_audit` under `strict` judges a page as `citations audit --strict` judges a bibliography.** A not found whose key is not on the checked list now fails it, as a disagreement does. The checked list is read from `<stem>.checked.txt` beside the bibliography, or from the new `checked` path parameter, and a listed key not found is reported under `marked` and as `checked_by_hand` on its row. `exit` and `outcome` give what the CLI would exit: `failed` (1), `could_not_check` (2) when only lookups failed or nothing was checked, and `passed` (0). The list's path, SHA-256 and stale keys are under `checked_list`, and the list's warnings under `warnings`. Without `strict`, `passed` is unchanged.
