**[reproducible-science] A quotation occurring more than once is reported as ambiguous.** `citations` returns `ambiguous` for a passage found twice, and 0.4.2 had no mapping for it, so `repro verify` reported `evidence.error ... KeyError: 'ambiguous'`. It is now `extraction=invalid` with reason `quotation_ambiguous`, flattening to `not_found`, the stages an ambiguous row selector takes. It is kept apart from `passage_ambiguous`, which says a document states two different numbers: a passage written twice contradicts nothing, and the fix is a `prefix` or `suffix` in the record.
