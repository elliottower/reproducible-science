**`repro verify` finds the preregistrations `prereg check` finds.** It looked for `PREREG.md` three levels down at most and never for a registration frozen by a commit line, so a nested plan or a commit-pinned registration that `prereg check` reported changed was "not used here" and the run exited 0. It now asks `prereg` which registrations a project holds, and a note `prereg check` raises without failing, such as a freeze held to an access floor lowered after the fact, is shown under its pass.
