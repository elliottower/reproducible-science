**`repro venue acm` drafts an Artifact Appendix from what the repository records.** The fields are those of the Unified Artifact Appendix template ACM conferences use for artifact evaluation, copied into the package with the commit of the template they were read at. A field a record bears on carries one line per fact, each naming its record: the commands under `regenerations`, the duration and environment of the last re-run in `.repro/reproductions.jsonl`, the claims in `repro.yaml` and the ledger, the plans frozen with `prereg`, the DOI and license in `CITATION.cff`, `LICENSE`, the lockfiles at the top of the project and the `origin` remote. Each such field is marked DRAFT with the records it came from; every other field is marked TODO and left for the authors, and no field is answered yes or no. Output is Markdown, LaTeX in the template's layout, or JSON, and `--output` never replaces a file without `--force`.
