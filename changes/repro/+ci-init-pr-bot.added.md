**`repro ci init --pr-bot` writes the pull-request bot's two caller workflows.** `repro-pr-bot.yml` runs on `pull_request` with `contents: read`, and `repro-pr-bot-respond.yml` on `workflow_run` after it and on new comments; both call the reusable workflow published with `reproducible-science-pr-bot`, and grant nothing at their top. A file this command did not write is never replaced, and `--force` replaces the two it wrote.
