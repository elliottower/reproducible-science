**`repro venue neurips` and `repro venue acl` draft the NeurIPS Paper Checklist and the ACL Responsible NLP Research checklist from the records.** The question text is stored verbatim in the package with its source and retrieval date: the sixteen questions of `checklist.tex` in the NeurIPS 2026 formatting archive, and the questions of the ARR checklist page. A NeurIPS answer stays `\answerTODO{}`, since each asks what the paper discloses, except open access (5): where the project's own DOI, Software Heritage ID or code repository is recorded it is `\answerYes{}` followed by a printed mark, `(DRAFT: from CITATION.cff; author: confirm this repository is public)`. No ACL answer is chosen. Where a record bears on a question (claims, declared commands and their re-runs, frozen plans, the DOI, origins, lockfiles, the license, the documentation), the justification is a draft marked DRAFT with the record named; every other question is left TODO. NeurIPS drafts are Markdown, LaTeX in the shape of `checklist.tex`, or JSON; ACL drafts are Markdown or JSON.
