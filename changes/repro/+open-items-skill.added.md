**The plugin has an `open-items` skill: the procedure an agent follows to fill the items `claims from-codecheck` and `claims from-corebench` leave open.** It proposes each binding and quotation as an assertion marked `basis: inferred`, using `claims verify`, `claims why`, `claims open-items`, `citations pin` and `shasum` and no other command, and reports the counts `claims open-items --format json` gives before and after, saved beside the draft. It forbids editing `open-items.json`, deleting a claim to close an item, removing `basis: inferred`, changing `reported` to make a check pass, editing a pinned quotation, running anything in a capsule or bundle, and calling anything verified that no command reported so, or any assertion it wrote verified at all.

An item's `unique` candidate may be proposed only after the manuscript's row and column, or the question's words, are checked against the address's labels; where the labels do not settle it, such as AUROC against accuracy, the agent says so and leaves the item open. The agent never writes `reviewed`, which is an author's acceptance.
