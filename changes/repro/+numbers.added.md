**`repro numbers` counts every number the manuscript prints and says which a declared assertion accounts for.** `repro verify` reports on the claims a manifest declares, and the author chose which to declare. `repro numbers` reads the text the claims read their sentences from, each artifact a `quote` reads beside an assertion of another kind or a prose address reads, or the ones named with `--text`, and gives every number in it one standing. It is `bound` where the occurrence lies in the passage of a `quote` whose claim carries that value as `reported`, digit for digit at the printed precision, or is the value a prose address selects. It is `set aside by rule` where a named rule reads the digits as something other than a quantity: `link`, `date`, `name`, `version or section`, `citation`, `label`, `list marker`, `ordinal`, `year`, `the word one`. It is `set aside with a reason` where the manifest lists its passage under the new `set_aside`, whose entries each name an `artifact`, the passage `text` and a required `reason`. Otherwise it is `unbound`, printed with its line and the words around it. The same digits reported by a claim about another passage bind nothing, and are marked `reported elsewhere`. The summary carries its denominator and the four counts add up to it, `8 numbers in 1 text: 1 bound, 3 set aside by rule, 1 set aside with a reason, 3 unbound`, with a count per rule and per `set_aside` entry. `--format json` prints every occurrence with its standing. It executes nothing, writes nothing and exits 0 whatever it counts, 2 with no manifest or no text to read, and `repro verify`, `repro stale` and `repro why` report as before.
