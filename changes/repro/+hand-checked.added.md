**`repro verify` says how many quotations were checked by hand.** Where `citations verify` read a list of quotations compared against the rendered page, the citations line reads `passed; checked by hand: <n> of <quotations> passages`, `--format json` carries the list under the citations section's `account.checked_by_hand`, and `--format sarif` puts the counts and the lists' digest under `checks.citations_checked_by_hand` in the run's property bag. The verdict is the one `citations verify` computed.
