**An `aggregate` assertion checks a printed value against a statistic over every run it names.** A sentence reporting a mean over ten seeds could be bound only with a `metric` on one file, which passes whichever of the ten runs that file is and reads none of the others. `kind: aggregate` takes `name`, `reported`, a `statistic` from a closed set (`mean`, `median`, `min`, `max`, `sum`, `count`), `members`, each an artifact and a locator, and a required `count` that must equal the number of members. A manifest stating `count: 10` and listing nine members does not load, and neither does one listing a member twice, so a run dropped from the list cannot leave a mean over the rest. The statistic is computed exactly, over rationals, and rounded once to the precision the manuscript prints: members holding `0.264` and `0.099` have a mean of `0.1815`, which is `0.182` at three places, where floating point prints `0.181`. `mode` and `tolerance` apply as for a `metric`. Every member must hold a number. A member whose file is missing leaves the assertion `unchecked`, one whose address does not resolve or holds no number makes it `not_found`, the detail names each such member, `member 4 of 10 (seed-3): /accuracy does not resolve in seed-3.json`, and the statistic of the remaining members is never compared. `repro verify` prints `accuracy: mean of 10 = 0.9137`; `repro why` lists each member's pin and stored value and then `the mean of the 10 values: 0.9137` above the value the manuscript prints; `repro stale` treats every member's file as read by the claim; and the SARIF result is located at each file. In `--format json` a decision carries `members`, each with its artifact digest, locator digest and extracted value, with `statistic` and `computed`. The set has no standard deviation. The existing kinds, and their output, are unchanged.
