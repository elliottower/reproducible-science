**`repro verify` and `repro ci` count a quotation `citations verify` reports `corrected` apart from `found`**, and `repro verify` grades it under the new `Policy.quotation_corrected`: a warning under `publication` and `strict`, ignored under `exploratory`, and where a project makes it an error the citations line says `FAILED under policy`. Under every policy the text report names each corrected quotation with the warnings its correction raised, such as `correction adds a negation` or `correction changes a number`, and the `citations verify` command that shows what each correction changes; `--format json` carries them under `citations.account.corrected`.
