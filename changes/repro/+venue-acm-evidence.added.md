**`repro venue acm-evidence` lists the records bearing on each criterion of ACM Artifact Review and Badging v1.1.** Every format is labelled "Evidence mapped to ACM Artifact Review and Badging v1.1 criteria; not an ACM badge." Under the policy's own text for each criterion it lists the findings that bear on it: a DOI or Software Heritage ID among the project's own identifiers for archival availability, never the origin of a copied file; the declared commands, their last re-runs, the claims and the documentation for an exercisable artifact; and those with the license and lockfiles for reuse. Each criterion's standing is `records listed`, `no record found` or `not determinable by this tool`, and the two criteria about results obtained by others are always the last. It does not say a criterion is met, and uses no badge name or image as a label or status. Output is HTML or JSON. The policy page was saved from a browser, since acm.org refuses scripted requests; each quoted criterion is stored with the page's address, the retrieval date, its line in the saved text and its sha256.
