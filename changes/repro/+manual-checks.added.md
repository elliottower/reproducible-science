**`repro verify` carries manual checks in its JSON and SARIF, and says nothing about them in its text.** Where `citations verify` read a list of manual checks, `--format json` has it under the citations section's `account.manual_checks`, and `--format sarif` puts the counts covered, stale, unknown and beside no claims file, the counts by computed outcome, and the lists' digest under `checks.citations_manual_checks` in the run's property bag. The verdict and the exit code are the ones `citations verify` computed.
