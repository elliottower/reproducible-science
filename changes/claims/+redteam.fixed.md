**A `reported` value is compared with the sentence it stands for, and a loose comparison is noted.** A manifest reporting 0.4213, for a claim whose text and manuscript both printed 0.9913, verified under every policy, because the file held 0.4213 and the printed number was never read. Each assertion with a `reported` value now carries a warning where the claim's text, or a passage its own quotations read, prints numbers and none agrees with `reported` at the coarser precision (`reported_not_in_claim`, `reported_not_in_manuscript`); where printed precision matched a `reported` of under two significant digits, or rounded across 0.05 (`coarse_precision`, `rounding_crosses_threshold`), which a mismatch never carries; and where a tolerance is more than a tenth of the value (`loose_tolerance`). Policies grade `reported_not_in_claim` by `reported_mismatch`, a warning under `exploratory` and `publication` and an error under `strict`, and the others by `reported_consistency`, a warning under every policy: a quoted passage prints other numbers than the one checked, or a count as a word (`no source`), and `0.03` is the right print of a stored `0.0314`, so none of them says the comparison is wrong. `verify` also counts the manuscript's unbound numbers as `repro numbers` does and reports them as one warning; `--max-unbound N` fails where there are more than N. Where the manifest names a manuscript and neither the claim's text nor a passage it quotes or a value its prose addresses select prints a number, the assertion carries `reported_uncompared`, a warning under every policy.
