**An artifact can be marked `basis: inferred`, and every assertion that reads one counts as inferred, whatever it says of itself, until the artifact is reviewed.** A review, on an assertion or an artifact, may now say `how` it was given and carry a `digest`, the sha256 of what was accepted with its basis and review left out; a review with a digest counts only while the assertion or artifact still has that digest, so changing a binding undoes its review. A review written without one counts as before.
