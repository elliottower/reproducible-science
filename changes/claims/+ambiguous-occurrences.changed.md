**Occurrences of one anchor pair that are the same number are one value, and `passage_ambiguous` says where each value sits.** `8.3` and `8.30`, `18` and `eighteen` under `cardinal_word`, and `1,062` and `1062` under `grouped` now resolve to one value, the finest spelling, where they were `passage_ambiguous`; different numbers, `8.3` and `8.34`, still are. The detail of `passage_ambiguous` gives each occurrence with its line and the words around it. A grouped number read under `decimal` is still `value_not_numeric`, and its detail now says to declare `form: grouped`. A correspondence of three sides or more records every pair that met an exact tie in a new `ties` field on the decision, with the names of the two sides; `tie` is the first of them, and a decision with no such pairs serializes as before.
