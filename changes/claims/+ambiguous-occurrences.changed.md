**Occurrences of one anchor pair that are the same number are one value, and `passage_ambiguous` says where each value sits.** `8.3` and `8.30`, `18` and `eighteen` under `cardinal_word`, and `1,062` and `1062` under `grouped` now resolve to one value, what the first occurrence prints, where they were `passage_ambiguous`; different numbers, `8.3` and `8.34`, still are. A correspondence compares each spelling at its own precision and names it by its line. The detail of `passage_ambiguous` gives each occurrence with its line and the words around it. With no `after`, a full stop closing the value after a digit is not read, so `8.3.` is `8.3`. A grouped number read under `decimal` is still `value_not_numeric`, and its detail now says to declare `form: grouped`; a grouped value is shown as printed. Two sides of a correspondence that select one place in one artifact, through locators differing only in `form` or in anchors one of which contains the other, are `unchecked` with the new reason `sides_read_one_place`. A correspondence of three places or more records every tied pair in `ties` and every disagreeing pair in `disagreements`, and `repro why` prints each; a decision with neither serializes as before.
