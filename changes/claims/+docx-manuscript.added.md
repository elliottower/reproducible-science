**A Word manuscript is checked as a text manuscript is.** `repro verify` resolves a `quote` and a prose address against a `.docx`, and `repro numbers` counts and binds the numbers it prints, table cells included. The text is the one `citations` reads a `.docx` source with, its `docx-text` extractor: body paragraphs in document order, then each table one row per line. Headers, footers, footnotes, comments and text boxes are not read. A `.docx` holding a tracked change of text is `unchecked` as unreadable, because `python-docx` reads neither the inserted nor the deleted text. A decision on a `.docx` names `python-docx` and its version as its tool, where it named `pdftotext`; one on a workbook names `openpyxl` (`.xlsx`, `.xlsm`) or `xlrd` (`.xls`), and one on a `.csv` names `citations`, whose `sheet-rows` reads it. `pip install "claims-cli[docx]"` or `"reproducible-science[docx]"` installs the reader.
