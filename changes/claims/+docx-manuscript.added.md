**A Word manuscript is checked as a text manuscript is.** `repro verify` resolves a `quote` and a prose address against a `.docx`, and `repro numbers` counts and binds the numbers it prints, table cells, footnotes and headers included. The text is the one `citations` reads a `.docx` source with, `docx-text` version 2. A decision on a `.docx` names `citations` and its version as its tool, where it named `pdftotext`; one on a workbook names `openpyxl` (`.xlsx`, `.xlsm`) or `xlrd` (`.xls`), and one on a `.csv` names `citations`, whose `sheet-rows` reads it. `repro numbers` sets aside the number after `endnote` as a label, as it does after `footnote`.
