**`claims open-items` counts the attempts recorded at each item in `open-items.attempts.jsonl`, and flags an item with more than five.** The record sits beside the open-items file, one proposal a line, appended to by `repro mcp` and never rewritten. The JSON gives `attempts` per item and `over_attempt_limit`; the text gives one line per item tried, with "check the binding is the claim's own value, not a search" past five. A match after many attempts is still counted as an inferred match, and nothing about the count changes the check.
