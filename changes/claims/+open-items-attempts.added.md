**`claims open-items` counts the attempts at each item from `.repro/attempts.jsonl` beside the open-items file, and flags an item tried more than five times.** The record is a hash chain written with `provenance_core.chain`, one event a proposal with the item, the attempt's number, the assertion and the outcome, appended by `repro mcp` and never rewritten. The JSON gives `attempts` per item, `over_attempt_limit` and `attempt_record` with whether the chain verifies; the text gives one line per item tried, with "check the binding is the claim's own value, not a search" past five, and says when the chain does not verify. A match after many attempts is still an inferred match.
