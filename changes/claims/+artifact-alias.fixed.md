**Two artifact ids for one file are a policy finding, and a correspondence over them is not compared with itself.** Two artifacts whose paths resolve to one file, or whose files hold the same bytes, now carry `same_path_as` or `same_digest_as` on their `ArtifactState`, and a policy reports each pair once as `artifact.alias`, naming both ids: a warning under `exploratory` and `publication`, an error under `strict`. A correspondence whose sides read one place through two such ids is `sides_read_one_place`, as through one id. Where one side's anchors also match places the other does not read, the pair is still refused, and the detail now says which place is shared, which other places the broader side matches, and which side to narrow.
