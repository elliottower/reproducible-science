**`repro verify` and `claims verify` with no `repro.yaml` report what they could check, and write nothing.** Discovery is the one `repro manifest init` uses: the result files under `results/`, `paper/artifacts/`, `outputs/` and `data/`, grouped by format, and the manuscript where exactly one `paper`, `manuscript` or `main` `.md`, `.tex` or `.txt` exists. Each number the manuscript prints that `repro numbers` would count as unbound, written in digits with two digits or a decimal point, is compared with every number a discovered JSON or YAML file holds, in mappings and lists, and every cell of a discovered CSV, TSV, PSV or columnar table, at the precision printed, and with a percent sign also as a percentage of the stored value. The comparison is `claims.verify.agrees`, which numeric assertions now use too. A column or a repeated list key that is index-like, by its name (`step`, `epoch`, `iter`, `iteration`, `idx`, `index`, `row`, `id`, `seed`, or none) or by values that step by one constant amount over at least ten rows, has its guesses listed last, tagged, and counted apart from the headline; nothing is hidden. One number agreeing with several rows of a column, or one key of several list elements, is one guess, shown at a place holding the number exactly where one does, with the other rows and their values. Guesses in JSON and YAML files come before guesses in tables. Each guess shows its line, the file, its locator and the stored value. At most 10,000 numbers are read from one file, and a delimited file is read no further than that; at most 1,000,000 comparisons are made. A note says where either limit was reached, and the headline counts the numbers compared and how many were not. A file that cannot be read is a note. The report ends `verified: 0` and names `repro manifest init`, and the command exits 2 as before, since nothing was checked. `--format json` prints one document with a `schema_version` of `reproducible.science/survey/0.1-experimental`, and `--format sarif` a run with no results, a warning notification that nothing was verified, and the survey in the run's properties.
