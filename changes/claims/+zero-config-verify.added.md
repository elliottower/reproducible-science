**`repro verify` and `claims verify` with no `repro.yaml` report what they could check, and write nothing.** Discovery is the one `repro manifest init` uses: the result files under `results/`, `paper/artifacts/`, `outputs/` and `data/`, grouped by format, and the manuscript where exactly one `paper`, `manuscript` or `main` `.md`, `.tex` or `.txt` exists. Each number the manuscript prints that `repro numbers` would count as unbound, written in digits with two digits or a decimal point, is compared with every number a discovered JSON or YAML file holds and every cell of a discovered CSV, TSV, PSV or columnar table, at the precision printed, and with a percent sign also as a percentage of the stored value. The comparison is `claims.verify.agrees`, which numeric assertions now use too. Each agreement is listed as a guess with its line, the file, its locator and the stored value. At most 10,000 numbers are read from one file and 1,000,000 comparisons made, and a note says where either limit was reached. The report ends `verified: 0` and names `repro manifest init`, and the command exits 2 as before, since nothing was checked. `--format json` prints the survey as one document, and `--format sarif` a run with no results, a warning notification that nothing was verified, and the survey in the run's properties.
