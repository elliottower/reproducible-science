**An assertion can be marked `basis: inferred`, and `claims open-items` counts what a reader of another tool's files left open.** An assertion proposed by a program or a model and not accepted by an author carries `basis: inferred`; accepting it is deleting the field, and a manifest that marks none dumps and hashes as before. An inferred assertion is checked as any other and is never counted as verified: `verify` counts it under `inferred_` and its outcome, prints `<inferred>` beside it and a line ending `0 inferred counted as verified`, and a policy grades it under the new `inferred` severity (an error under `publication` and `strict`, a warning under `exploratory`). The check record carries `assertion.basis: inferred`, and `read_outcome` reads a `match` on one as `not-checked`, so no exporter passes it on as a match; the record's JSON Schema is regenerated with the field. A reader writes a draft manifest, `repro.draft.yaml`, and `open-items.json` beside it, listing per claim what its source does not state, with any candidate a search of the files found kept out of the draft. `claims open-items open-items.json --format json` checks the draft and counts each claim once: bound from the source alone, every item filled by assertions that matched, filled by one that did not, still open, or outside the items; with the items by how they were filled and the assertions by basis and outcome.

**`reviewed: {who, when}` records who accepted an inferred assertion.** The assertion is then counted as declared everywhere and the record carries `assertion.reviewed`; on a declared assertion the field is refused. An inferred assertion nobody reviewed fails `publication` and `strict`. An open item may carry `unique: true`, where exactly one of its candidates passes the reader's guards (listed first), or a `reason` where none does; no candidate is ever written into a draft. `claims open-items` counts items with a unique candidate, several, one that fails the guards, or none, and items open, filled, and filled and passing.

**An inferred match is never shown as a pass or as verified.** `claims verify --format sarif` gives it `kind: notApplicable` under `repro/unchecked`, with `basis` among the properties; `claims why` says `matched, inferred: not counted as verified`, counts it apart from the declared outcomes (`inferred` in `--format json`), and lists it under `not established`. An open-items file names its draft by a file name in its own directory, and no other.
