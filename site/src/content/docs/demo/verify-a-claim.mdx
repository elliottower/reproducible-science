A manuscript prints a number. The claim it belongs to names a run, and the run names its
outputs by the hashes they had when they were recorded. This notebook walks that chain and
then breaks it in four ways — a contradicted number, an address that resolves to nothing, an
artifact edited after it was pinned, a deleted ledger entry — and reports what each break
produces. Every cell runs the published package, and nothing leaves the browser.
