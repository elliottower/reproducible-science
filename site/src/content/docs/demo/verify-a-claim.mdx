title: Verify a claim
description: Seal a run, bind a manuscript claim to it, and break each link in turn
---

A number in a manuscript names a claim. The claim names a run. The run names its outputs,
hashed when they were recorded.

This notebook walks that chain forward and then breaks it — a contradicted number, an address
that resolves to nothing, an artifact edited after it was pinned, a deleted ledger entry —
and shows which kind of failure each one produces.

Every cell runs the published package. Nothing is uploaded and there is no account to create.
